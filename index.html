Welcome To RNS Tours & Travels

With immense pleasure, we introduce RNS Tours & Travels, based out of Bengaluru. We specialize in group tours and customized holiday packages to some of the most famous heritage destinations across India.

Our passion is to showcase India's rich cultural heritage while providing the best travel experience. We offer carefully handcrafted tour packages giving deep insights into India's diverse culture, traditional cuisine, historical monuments, and architectural wonders.

In addition to domestic tours, we provide a wide range of international travel packages that can be fully customized to suit your preferences, budget, and travel needs. At RNS Tours & Travels, we believe every journey should create unforgettable memories.

Our Story

A Bengaluru Legacy Since 2004

RNS Tours & Travels is a Bengaluru-based travel company serving travelers since 2004. We are committed to creating memorable travel experiences through personalized services, carefully planned itineraries, and exceptional customer support.

Born from a deep love for India's rich cultural tapestry, we began with a mission to make heritage travel accessible, meaningful, and truly unforgettable. Over two decades, we have grown into a full-service travel agency offering domestic, international, and specialized niche travel.

Our team of experienced travel advisors brings deep destination knowledge, strong vendor relationships, and a genuine passion for creating journeys that transform travelers.
